# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want NexTube to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that makes the community better:

- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Helping newcomers. Everyone started somewhere
- Focusing on what is best for the users and the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they
deem harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/mehefujali/nex-tube/security/advisories/new) or by
messaging a maintainer on GitHub. All reports are handled confidentially.
